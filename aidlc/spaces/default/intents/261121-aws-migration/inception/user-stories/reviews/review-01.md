### Summary

The stories are well formed: each has an actor, an action and a value, the criteria are in Given/When/Then and observable, and the deliberate additions are disclosed. Three problems keep the set from being implementable without questions. FR10.3's name and outside-the-tree cases have no criteria. The maintainer's view of accounts conflicts with the no-email rule. The FR4.4 departure and the added items are not carried into the requirements and traceability. The remaining findings are smaller gaps in corner cases.
