### Summary

The catalogue is structurally sound and the diagram and tables agree with it, but the design leaves four things unowned or unspecified: the switch story (US8.4) has no component that can do it, the migration import path has no identity, limit or record rules, the daily upload limit can be defeated by parallel starts, and account removal does not reach the sign-in service's copy of the email. Fixing these four is needed before a developer could build from this without guessing; the Minor items can be settled at functional design.
