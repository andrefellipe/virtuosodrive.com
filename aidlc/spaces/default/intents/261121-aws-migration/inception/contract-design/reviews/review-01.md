### Summary

Thirteen contracts cover every declared edge and the ten settlements are mostly sound and declared, but two code-level dependencies are missing: U3 needs U2 and U4 without a declared edge. The session, caching, upload-reservation and name-uniqueness behaviours also have gaps that a developer would have to decide alone. These are fixable by editing the contract document, so the main concern is clarity, not redesign.
