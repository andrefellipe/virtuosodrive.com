### Summary

The documents are thorough and measurable, but three architectural defects make them not yet buildable. The pull-request role can read state that may hold secrets. The deploy roles have no environment or privilege limits in a single account. The per-address wrong-password limit (NFR2.3) has no mechanism in the chosen stack and no cost. The minor items (a 30-second contradiction, mis-filed NFR numbers, unapproved tools, cost items hidden behind the US$50 ceiling) should be fixed in the same revision.
